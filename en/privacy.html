Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what data Jurnal Koneksi (“Jurkon”, “we”) collects, what it is used for, who can see it, and what you can do with your data. We have written it as briefly and clearly as we can.

1. In short

  • We only keep the data needed to run the service.
  • Your data is not sold, and Jurkon shows no ads and uses no ad trackers or third-party analytics tools.
  • Your email and direct messages are not shown to other users.
  • You can download all your data and delete your account at any time from Settings on the Profile page.

2. Data we collect

Account data. Email address and password (stored in encrypted form by the authentication service; we cannot read your password). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and account identity from Google to create your account.

Profile data. The username, display name, bio, and profile photo you fill in yourself.

Content and activity. Posts and replies, likes (and when they were made), the list of accounts you follow, accounts you block, direct messages, reports you send, and your notifications.

View counts. So that every post can show a “views” count, we record which posts have been shown to you (once per person per post). This record is private and cannot be read by other users; only the total number is shown.

Technical data. Like most web services, our hosting and infrastructure providers may log IP addresses, browser type, and access times for security and to keep the service running. We do not use this data to build ad profiles.

3. What the data is used for

  • Creating and securing your account, and letting you sign in and recover your password.
  • Showing profiles, posts, likes, messages, and notifications.
  • Preventing spam and abuse: limits on posts, messages, likes, and reports; automatic checking of writing before it is sent; and review of reports.
  • Responding to questions, reports, or requests you send us.
  • Meeting legal obligations where required by the regulations that apply in Indonesia.

4. Who can see what

  • Profiles and posts: only Jurkon members who are signed in. People who are not signed in cannot read them, except for notes you yourself make public (see the next point).
  • Public notes (optional, per note): by default all your notes are private. If you make a note public, that note, your name, and your @username can be read by anyone through its link and can be indexed by search engines such as Google. Separately, you can tick a box so the note also appears on the public Explore page (jelajahi.html); without that tick, the note can only be read through its link. Notes that were already public before this feature existed do not appear in the list unless you tick the box yourself. You can make a note private again at any time; the link stops working immediately, but copies outside Jurkon (screenshots, search engine caches) cannot be taken back by us. Public notes must not contain links, emails, phone numbers, or @mentions, and administrators may revoke the public status of notes that break the rules.
  • Profile photo: stored in storage that can be opened through a direct link. Anyone with the link can open the image without signing in. Do not upload photos you do not want to share openly.
  • Follower and following lists: can be seen by signed-in members, including who follows whom.
  • Direct messages: can only be read by the two people in the conversation.
  • Email: not shown to other users.
  • Reports: can only be seen by administrators for review.

Please remember: anything you write in a post can be copied or screenshotted by other members, outside our control.

5. Third parties that help us

We use the following services to run Jurkon. They process data according to their function and are subject to their own policies:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and file storage (profile photos).
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of the site’s pages.
  • jsDelivr: delivery of JavaScript libraries (CDN).
  • Google Fonts: loading of fonts. When loading them, your browser contacts Google’s servers and your IP address is visible to Google.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: an anti-bot security check on the sign in and sign up pages. While the check runs, your browser contacts Cloudflare’s servers.

Some of these services may store data on servers outside Indonesia. By using Jurkon, you understand that your data may be processed abroad by these providers.

6. Data stored on your device

Jurkon does not use advertising cookies or trackers. To work, the app stores in your browser: your sign-in session, your display theme choice, your language choice, your feed choice (Public or Following), a temporary count of failed sign-in attempts (to slow down repeated password guessing), a temporary cache so pages load quickly and can open with a weak signal, and temporary markers for view counts and notifications. This data can be cleared through your browser settings or when you sign out.

To understand where new visitors drop off, Jurkon counts common steps (opening the landing page, pressing the start button, opening Explore, opening the sign in page) as daily totals per language in our own database. These counts contain no IP address, device ID, cookie, or data that could point to a single person, use no third-party analytics service, and are not recorded if the browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

7. How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data is kept while your account is active. You can delete a single post at any time. If you use Delete account in Settings, your profile, posts, likes, and the messages you sent are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Backup copies managed by the infrastructure provider may remain for a limited period before being deleted automatically. Reports needed to maintain security and legal compliance may be kept for as long as needed for those purposes.

8. Your rights over your data

Under the Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022), you have the right to:

  • know and access your personal data (use Download my data in Settings);
  • correct inaccurate data (change it through Edit profile);
  • delete your data (use Delete account);
  • withdraw consent and limit or object to certain processing;
  • lodge a complaint with us, or with the competent authority where needed.

For anything you cannot do yourself in the app, contact us using the details below. We try to respond within a reasonable time, generally no more than 14 days.

9. Security

We use database-level access restrictions, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and rate limiting to protect your data. Even so, no system is completely immune. Use a strong, unique password, do not share it with anyone, and contact us right away if you suspect your account is being misused. If a data breach occurs that puts you at risk, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

10. Age of users

Jurkon is intended for users aged 17 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you are a parent or guardian and know that your child has signed up, contact us so the account can be deleted.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of the page. For important changes, we will notify you through the app.
