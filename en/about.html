About Jurkon

Jurnal Koneksi (Jurkon) is a minimalist social network where you write short notes and connect with other people without the excess noise.

What we stand for

  • Short and clear. Notes are up to 250 characters, messages up to 750 characters.
  • Comfortable and safe. There is writing review, spam limits, reporting, and blocking.
  • No ads. We do not sell your data and do not use ad trackers.
  • You are in control. Download your data or delete your account at any time.

How it works

Jurkon is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with Supabase for the database and authentication, and is hosted on GitHub Pages. You can add it to your phone’s home screen like an app.
