Rules of use

Not allowed: spam, fraud, hate speech, harassment, adult content, threats, and sharing other people’s personal data. Posts and accounts that violate these rules may be removed. You can report posts and block other users at any time.

Jurkon checks writing before it is sent to keep things comfortable and in line with the laws that apply in Indonesia:

  • Cannot be sent: gambling promotion (online gambling, togel, slots), adult content and sexually vulgar words, drug sales, fraudulent offers or sales of personal data, threats against others, hate speech against ethnic, religious, racial, or social groups, and national ID (KTP/NIK) numbers.
  • Shown a warning: rude words and phone numbers. You can soften your writing or send it anyway, but a reported post can still be reviewed and removed.

Automatic checks can be wrong. If your reasonable writing is rejected, change the wording a little or contact us using the details below.

To prevent spam, the number of posts, messages, and reports per day is limited.

Privacy Policy (summary)

We store your email, profile, posts, likes, follow list, and messages to run the service. Your data is not sold. Your email is not shown to other users. Profiles and posts can only be seen by members who are signed in. Direct messages can only be read by the two people in the conversation. The full explanation is in the Privacy Policy.

Your data and account

On the Profile page you can download your data and delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, posts, likes, and the messages you sent.
