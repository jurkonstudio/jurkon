Frequently asked questions

What is Jurkon?

Jurkon (Jurnal Koneksi) is a social journal made in Indonesia. You write short notes of up to 250 characters, read others’ notes in a feed that has an end, and can leave a note for your future self.

Is Jurkon free?

Yes. Jurkon is free, has no ads, and never sells your data.

Can other people and Google read my notes?

By default all your notes are private. Only notes you choose to make public can be read by people without an account and by search engines, and you can make them private again at any time.

What is a time capsule?

A time capsule is a note locked until a date you choose, even from you. Like a letter to your future self.

Which languages are supported, and do I need to install an app?

Jurkon comes in seven languages: Indonesian, English, Malay, Javanese, Sundanese, Chinese, and Japanese. Just open it in your browser; if you like, add it to your home screen without an app store.
