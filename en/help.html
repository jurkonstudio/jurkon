Help Center

Answers to the questions that come up most often. If you can’t find yours, write to us at the very bottom.

Account

How do I sign up and sign in?

Open the Sign in page, then sign up with your email and a password, or choose to sign in with Google. Pick a username of 3–20 characters (lowercase letters, numbers, or underscores).

I forgot my password.

On the Sign in page, choose Forgot password, enter your email, then follow the link sent to that email. Check your spam folder if it hasn’t arrived within a few minutes.

How do I change my email?

Open Profile, tap the gear icon (Settings), fill in New email, then tap Change email. A confirmation link is sent to the new email (and may also go to the old one). The new email only takes effect after you click the link.

Why can’t I change my name, username, or photo?

Changes are limited per month so accounts can’t be used to impersonate or deceive. The limit resets on the 1st of each month, and your remaining allowance is shown on the Edit profile screen. A username can also only be changed once a day.

What do the blue and gold checkmarks mean?

A blue checkmark means the account has been reviewed and verified by the Jurkon team. A gold checkmark means an official account run directly by the Jurkon team. Press and hold the checkmark to see its description.

Writing and interacting

Why was my writing rejected or shown a warning?

Jurkon checks writing before it is sent. Content such as gambling promotion, adult content, fraud, threats, hate speech, and national ID (KTP/NIK) numbers cannot be sent, while rude words and phone numbers only get a warning. Automatic checks can be wrong, so try changing your wording a little. Details are on the Rules page.

Why do I see “Too fast” or “Daily limit reached”?

There are limits on the number of posts, replies, messages, and likes to prevent spam. Newer accounts (roughly the first 3 days) have stricter limits. Wait a moment or try again tomorrow.

How do I delete my post?

Tap the trash icon on your own post, then confirm. A deleted post cannot be restored.

What is the “views” count?

The number of different people who have seen a post. Each person is counted once, and your own posts are not counted.

What is the difference between the Public and Following feeds?

Public shows notes from all members. Following shows only your notes and those of the accounts you follow.

Safety and privacy

How do I report a problem post?

Tap the Report icon on the post, write your reason (up to 300 characters), then send. Reports are only seen by the administrators for review.

How do I block someone?

Open the person’s profile, tap the ⋯ menu, then choose block. Their posts will be hidden from you. To unblock, choose Unblock in the same menu.

Who can read my messages?

Only you and the person you are talking to. Your email is not shown to other users. More in the Privacy Policy.

How do I download my data?

Open Profile, tap the gear icon (Settings), then choose Download my data. You will get a single file containing your profile, posts, likes, and messages.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings then choose Delete account. You need to type HAPUS to confirm. Deletion is permanent: your profile, posts, likes, and the messages you sent are removed and cannot be recovered. It’s best to download your data first.

Appearance

How do I change the theme?

In Settings there are three theme options: Paper, Light, and Dark.

Can Jurkon be installed like an app?

Yes. In your phone’s browser, open the menu and choose Add to Home Screen or Install app.
